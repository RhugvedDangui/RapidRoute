E-COMMERCE · LOGISTICS (Rapid Route)

AI Logistics Optimizer for Small Sellers
Problem Statement

Small sellers lose margin because of inefficient logistics. They lack the tools that large sellers use for batching, route optimization, and delay prediction.

Objective

Build a system that optimizes delivery routes and order batching, predicts delays, and suggests cost-saving strategies tailored to small sellers.

Suggested Use Cases
Route Optimization

Compute efficient delivery routes for a day’s orders.

Order Batching

Group orders intelligently by location, time window, and size.

Delay Prediction

Flag orders likely to be delayed before they ship.

What to Build
Optimization logic, even if heuristic-based
Simple seller-facing UI with clear recommendations
Cost or time estimate for each suggested plan
Must Have
Working optimizer on a sample order set
Clear before-and-after metrics (cost, time, or distance)
Recommendations the seller can act on directly
